HIGHER / LOWER

Fifteen seconds. Your decision.

01 / COMPARE

Pick the higher value

Choose which footballer has the higher value in the bundled game dataset. Correct picks build your streak; values are game data, not live quotes.

02 / KEEP GOING

A fresh chance

A wrong answer or timeout costs one life. One life refills every ten minutes, up to three automatic lives. Your best streak stays on your device.

03 / PLAY YOUR WAY

English or Türkçe

English is the default. Switch to Turkish in the game. The Android build bundles player data and artwork so the core game works offline. Ads need a connection.

Still in the training ground.
